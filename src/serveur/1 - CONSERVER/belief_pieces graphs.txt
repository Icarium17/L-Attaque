Probability Distribution
For Every Hidden Enemy Piece


The opponent_belief_pieces_left dictionary acts as the global constraint
that connects every hidden piece together. Whenever a piece is revealed or
destroyed, every remaining BeliefPiece updates its probabilities to reflect



the new state of the enemy army.
